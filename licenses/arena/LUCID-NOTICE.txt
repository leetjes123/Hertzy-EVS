LUCID Arena SDK runtime
=======================

The camera edition of HertzyEVS contains runtime library files of the LUCID Arena SDK (Arena_*, GenTL_LUCID_*,
lucidlog_*, lucid_metavision_sdk_*, lucid_imx63x_plugin5 and the GenICam DLLs in app\).
